\section*{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Conclusion}

This document is a proposal. It should be peer-reviewed, revised further, and kept public and open-source. Contributors should understand that no document, however carefully designed, captures all of what justice requires or anticipates all the ways power will seek to assert itself.

Every person who reads this should attempt to identify what contributors have gotten wrong, what they have omitted, and what the foundational principles require that they have failed to specify.

The aim is a principled system -- one that places the freedom and dignity of every human being above the comfort of those who would hold power, and that gives every generation the authority and the tools to make its system more just than the last.
